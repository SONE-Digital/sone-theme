Why Choose Colorado M-Series?

UVgel Technology

Advanced UV gel ink technology delivers robust, fade-resistant prints with exceptional durability and color vibrancy.

White Ink Option

Optional white ink capability opens up new creative possibilities and expands your application range significantly.

FLXfinish+

Print matte, gloss, or mixed matte and gloss in one print. Achieve stunning visual effects with flexible finishing options.

Impressive Media Range

Compatible with a wide variety of media types, giving you the flexibility to handle diverse printing applications.

Upgradeable Functionality

Modular architecture allows you to start with basic configuration and upgrade as your business grows and requirements evolve.

High Reliability

Built for continuous operation with consistent, high-quality output and minimal maintenance requirements.
